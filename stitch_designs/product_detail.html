Living Sanctuary Care
Thrives in medium to bright indirect sunlight. Keep away from harsh midday direct sun which can scorch the fenestrated foliage. Excellent within 4-8 feet of an east or south-facing window.
Water once weekly or whenever the top 2 inches of soil feel completely dry to the touch. Yellowing lower leaves signal overwatering; drooping indicates it is ready for a thorough drink.
Ideal temperature between 65°F-85°F (18°C-30°C). Normal indoor ambient humidity is fine, but loves 50%-70% humidity. Mist leaves occasionally or wipe with a damp microfiber cloth to remove dust.
