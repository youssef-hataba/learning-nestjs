## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a nocturnal botanical aesthetic—luxurious, serene, and deeply rooted in living nature. It pairs deep shaded forest tones with sharp, luminous botanical accents to transform indoor horticulture into an elevated lifestyle ritual. The interface evokes the tranquility of a curated glasshouse conservatory at midnight, marrying organic life with architectural minimalism.

The visual direction centers on **Biophilic Glassmorphism**:
- Ultra-dark, warm chlorophyll canvas foundations layered with translucent smoked-glass containers.
- Dramatic cutouts and asymmetrical organic contours that allow photorealistic botanical subjects to break frame boundaries.
- Luminous chartreuse and electric lime micro-interactions providing radiant visual contrast without overwhelming the grounded earthiness.
- High-contrast, clean sans-serif typography ensuring effortless commerce navigation and editorial poise.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep biophilic shadows punctuated by chlorophyll illumination:

- **Primary (`#B6F076`)**: Luminous sprout chartreuse used for hero CTA fills, critical active indicators, section bracket accents, and focus highlights.
- **Secondary (`#2E4732`)**: Muted pine-moss green serving as subtle borders, interactive hover states, secondary surface backdrops, and active toggle outlines.
- **Tertiary (`#9BE650`)**: Vivid leaf lime applied to product rating stars, micro-badges, and interactive micro-animations.
- **Neutral (`#131A13`)**: The abyssal canopy dark base tone. Canvas backgrounds sit between `#121812` and `#1B231B`, creating high perceived depth behind translucent cards.
- **Surface Accents & Glass Overlays**: Smoked glass fills render at `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` with linear glass borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` fading to `rgba(182, 240, 118, 0.12)`).
- **Text & Foreground**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for primary titles, softened Sage Milk (`#D2DDD2`) for body descriptions, and Muted Leaf (`#7E927E`) for secondary labels and pricing denominators.

## Typography

The typography uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve a contemporary, welcoming, and geometric aesthetic. The typeface's wide apertures and clean terminals guarantee pristine readability on dark translucent backgrounds while preserving a sculpted, architectural feel.

- **Editorial Section Headers**: Section titles are accented with distinctive architectural frame brackets (`[ Our Top Selling ]`), set in medium weight with balanced tracking to signal intentional curation.
- **Price Displays**: Currency markings (`Rs.`, `$`) and amounts utilize medium-bold tracking at `headline-sm` to `headline-md` weights, ensuring effortless skimming on product cards.
- **Legibility**: Avoid pure `#FFFFFF` for continuous multi-line paragraphs; use muted sage (`#C4D1C4`) to eliminate dark-mode halation and optical eye fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a rhythmic 12-column fluid grid system across desktop viewports, transitioning to an 8-column layout for tablets and a 4-column system for mobile devices.

- **Vertical Cadence**: Generous section separation (`space-xl` multiplied for hero bands) provides breathing room for oversized botanical photography to overlap container boundaries without visual clutter.
- **Card Framing**: Product cards use asymmetric inner padding (`space-lg` bottom and sides, augmented top clearance) so floating plant silhouettes can physically overhang the card boundary by 32px to 64px.
- **Responsive Adaptations**: On mobile breakpoints, side-by-side featured banners reflow into stacked cards with plant imagery centered directly above the textual details, preserving vertical flow and touch target ergonomics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows; instead, it utilizes **tonal glass layering**, fine light strokes, and blurred atmosphere:

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Deep organic dark background `#131A13`, occasionally enhanced with ambient radial glows (`radial-gradient(circle, rgba(46,71,50,0.3) 0%, transparent 70%)`).
- **Level 1 (Submerged Cards & Rows)**: Semi-translucent panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`) and a delicate 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- **Level 2 (Hovered & Active Overlays)**: Enhanced glass opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), border illumination shifting toward primary sprout lime (`rgba(182, 240, 118, 0.35)`), accompanied by a diffused outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 20px 0 rgba(182, 240, 118, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Action & Navigation)**: Floating pill navigation bars and checkout floating buttons apply `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`, higher surface opacity (`rgba(19, 26, 19, 0.85)`), and crisp high-contrast border outlines.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** geometry combined with sculpted botanical asymmetry:

- **Buttons & Micro-elements**: Full pill contours (`border-radius: 9999px`) for buttons, search inputs, rating tags, and navigation headers.
- **Glass Product Panels**: Generous rounded top caps and base corners (`rounded-xl`, 2rem to 3rem radius). Selected hero showcase cards feature asymmetric organic roundings (e.g., top-left and top-right heavily rounded into an arched conservatory silhouette).
- **Icon Capsules**: Add-to-bag triggers and carousel direction controls are housed in circular or rounded squircle enclosures (`12px` to `50%` radius) mirroring the softness of plant foliage.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Luminous Pill)**: Background `#B6F076`, label text `#121812` (bold 600 weight), fully rounded pill profile. Hover triggers subtle scale (`1.02`) and outer glow `0 0 16px rgba(182, 240, 118, 0.4)`.
- **Ghost Glass Pill**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`, label text `#FFFFFF`. Hover elevates border to `rgba(182, 240, 118, 0.6)`.
- **Icon Quick-Cart Button**: Circular or squircle pill button (`42px × 42px`) with frosted glass background, 1px border stroke, housing a minimalist bag icon. Used beside price labels.

### Product Display Cards
- **Curated Conservatory Cards**: Frosted container with top arched or rounded corners. Plant photography cuts out from the background and floats 40px over the top border. Contains plant taxonomy name, brief single-line botanical care note, price numerator (`Rs. 399/-`), and immediate circular cart icon action.

### Badges, Stars & Review Pills
- **Review Snippet Card**: Smoked glass horizontal card displaying circular user avatar, customer name, 5-star rating clustered using tertiary leaf lime (`#9BE650`), and excerpt testimonial in soft muted sage.
- **Section Indicator Bracket**: Unique textual accent consisting of lime-colored corner brackets (`┌ [ Title ] ┘`) wrapping header highlights.

### Input Fields & Newsletter Subscription
- **Glass Field Form**: Border 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`, dark moss background (`rgba(19, 26, 19, 0.6)`), pill shape. Attached integrated submit button filled in solid `#FFFFFF` or `#B6F076` with uppercase punchy typography.

### Navigation Header & Dock
- **Floating Island Header**: Contained within an ultra-slim pill bar spanning the top layout, featuring brand mark with leaf sprout glyph, text navigation links with active dot indicators, and right-aligned search, cart bag, and mobile hamburger controls.
